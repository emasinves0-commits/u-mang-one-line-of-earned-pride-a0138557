## Why this line

The field of 4,353 entries is flooded with the same moves: night shifts (97 titles), the stutterer reading aloud (62), "After N years of X, then Y" (57), violin practice before dawn (49), signing one's own name (44), the foreman's nod (40), the apprentice's weld (38). This line takes a different road:

- **A small person no leader covers.** A gravedigger — not a student, apprentice, or night-shift nurse — doing the day's last hard physical work.
- **The pride is felt in the body, never named.** The word "proud" never appears; the palms shaking until they stop *is* the pride, earned inside the line by the labor itself.
- **It lands on the last word.** The whole line is one scene seen happening — last plot, sapling set straight, soil pressed — and the shaking resolves only in the final words.
- **Fresh image.** Living growth planted over fresh ground, by the hands that dug it, is not the stock trophy, podium, or graduation.
